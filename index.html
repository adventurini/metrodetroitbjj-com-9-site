About

An academy built on lineage, integrity & community.

Welcome to the Metro Detroit Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Academy. We offer the highest quality BJJ instruction in a clean, friendly, respectful and family environment. Whether you are looking for discipline, fitness, sport or self defense training, we will meet your needs.

Our community-based method integrates beginners into ongoing classes with advanced students, providing a wider pool of knowledge, wisdom and technique. Advanced students, in turn, gain insight by helping beginners progress. MDBJJ is a proud affiliate of the De La Riva BJJ Association.

Programs

Training for every age, every goal, every level.

Adults · All Ages

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Gi / No Gi

An effective and sophisticated martial art specializing in ground fighting. Students learn control, escapes, armlocks, chokes, sweeps, reversals, takedowns, throws and leglocks — emphasizing grappling over striking.

Technique · Leverage · Competition
Ages 3–5

Little Lions

Fun games incorporating movements for coordination — the building blocks for all athletics and BJJ/wrestling. A playful first step onto the mats.

Coordination · Play · Foundation
Ages 6–7

Little Grapplers

Children play grappling games in which essential self-defense techniques are secretly taught — developing strong reflexes and learning leverage and control naturally.

Reflexes · Games · Technique
Ages 8–12

Junior BJJ

Since bullying is most prevalent in this age group, children learn non-violent techniques to defend themselves. No punches or kicks — instead, leverage is used to establish control until help arrives.

Bully Prevention · Confidence · Control
Kids

Kids BJJ / Bully Free

Simple yet effective skills appropriate for kids to use. Three objectives: boost confidence, increase awareness of physical and verbal bullying, and be ready for common contingencies.

Confidence · Awareness · Readiness
Adults · All Levels

BJJ All Levels & Open Mat

All-skill BJJ classes plus dedicated Open Mat for MDBJJ members — refine your game, drill positions and roll with a wider pool of training partners.

Drilling · Rolling · Community

Head Instructor

Scott Zapczynski

4th Degree Black Belt · Owner & Head Instructor

Lineage

Certified 4th Degree Black Belt under 8th Degree Red/White Belt Master Ricardo De La Riva — Copacabana, Rio de Janeiro.

"The knowledge, leadership, integrity and loyalty taught and shown to me by Master De La Riva are what I pass on to every student at Metro Detroit BJJ."

Scott Zapczynski is the owner and head instructor of the Metro Detroit Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Academy. He began his BJJ training in 1996 and was blessed with the opportunity to spend four months each year for over ten years living in Rio de Janeiro, becoming fluent in Portuguese and training daily at Master De La Riva's academy in Copacabana.

Scott is the only American to have been awarded his blue, purple, brown and black belts directly from Master De La Riva at his academy in Rio de Janeiro. He honed his skills training privately with fellow De La Riva Black Belt and Brazilian National Champion Marcelo Grosso, and Master De La Riva himself.

Over the years, Scott has assisted Master De La Riva in teaching BJJ and Self Defense seminars across the USA and Brazil, and has gained unique insight from training inside the pre-fight camps of UFC champions Anderson Silva, Rodrigo "Minotauro" Nogueira, Lyoto Machida and BJ Penn.

Scott grew up in Shelby Township and is a graduate of Eisenhower High School and Michigan State University.
